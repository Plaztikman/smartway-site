Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

We keep the information we hold about you to the minimum needed to sell you the game and check your licence.

When you buy

Lemon Squeezy, our reseller, collects your name, email address, billing details and payment information to process your order. We receive your name, email address and order details from them. We never see your full card details. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy explains how it handles your data.

Your licence

We store your licence key with a scrambled (hashed) copy of your email address, and once you activate, your email address, the activation date and an anonymous ID for your PC. The PC ID is a one-way code made from your computer's hardware ID; it can't be used to identify you or your computer elsewhere. This data is held in Google Firebase.

When the game checks your licence, it sends only your licence key. No gameplay data, files or personal information leave your PC.

Why we use it

To deliver your licence key, activate and check your licence, prevent misuse, and answer support emails. The lawful basis is performing our contract with you.

How long we keep it

For as long as your licence exists, plus any period the law requires for sales records.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how it's used. Email us at plaztikman@live.com. Deleting your licence data means the game can no longer be activated. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This website doesn't use cookies or tracking. The checkout pages are run by Lemon Squeezy.
